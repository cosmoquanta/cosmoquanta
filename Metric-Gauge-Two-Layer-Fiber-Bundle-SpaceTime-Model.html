Abstract:

This chapter aims to establish a unified framework that integrates general relativity (GR) and quantum field theory (QFT), aligning with observed physical phenomena. The proposed model is based on a quantum network capable of conducting electromagnetic waves with quantized energy, which must achieve spatial isotropy and Lorentz covariance. A potential approach involves the random polycrystalline stacking of discrete space elementary quanta (SEQ) at the Planck scale, forming a topologically homogeneous 3D structure with defects.

The chapter further explores how quantum spin can decouple from spatial elasticity to ensure that spin degrees of freedom are not impeded by spatial elasticity. This is crucial for maintaining the independence of quantum spin properties within the elastic framework of space.

Additionally, the integration of thermodynamic time into the evolutionary model is discussed. Time is defined through the counting process of transformations within the SEQ network, reflecting the finite and discrete nature of time due to the limited number of transformations possible within a finite universe.

The harmonization of general relativity's geometric interpretation with quantum field theory's quantum spin and resonance under space deformation will be further elaborated in the following chapters. Specifically, the modulation of time under spatial deformation will be explored in detail, offering a foundational bridge between the geometric perspective of general relativity and the quantum description of physical phenomena.

Likewise, the generation of gravity by mass, where quantum field theory provides insights into the origin of mass and general relativity interprets gravity through the geometric deformation of spacetime, will be discussed in subsequent sections. These discussions aim to lay the groundwork for constructing a spatial structure platform that can seamlessly incorporate both general relativity (GR) and quantum field theory (QFT), ultimately contributing to a unified understanding of the cosmos.
